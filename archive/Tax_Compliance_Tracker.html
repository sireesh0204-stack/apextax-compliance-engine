Form 1065: Partnership & Real Estate Core Pass-Through Core
  • G&A Unbundling: Statutory split between Page 1 Line 9 salaries, Line 10 guaranteed payments, and Line 20 itemized statement. (IRC §162)
  • M-1 Book-to-Tax Reconciliations: 50% non-deductible meals (§274(n)), penalties (§162(f)), tax-exempt municipal interest (§103), and MACRS depreciation.
  • Form 8825 (Rental Real Estate): Reporting gross rental receipts, operating expenses, and MACRS straight-line depreciation; netting to Schedule K Line 2.
  • Schedule L Balance Tie-Out: Total Assets (Line 14) ties 100% to Total Liabilities & Partners' Capital (Line 22) for BOY and EOY.
  • Schedule M-2 Roll-Forward: Line 1 BOY + Line 3 Book Net Income – Line 6 Cash Distributions = Line 9 Ending Capital (ties to Sch L Line 21).
  • IRC §752 Debt Allocation: 100% recourse debt to General Partner (economic risk of loss); $0 to Limited Partner (Treas. Reg. §1.752-2).
  • Qualified Nonrecourse Debt: Allocation of commercial mortgages on real estate across all partners by profit share (IRC §465(b)(6)).
  • Outside Basis Tie-Out: Schedule K-1 Item L Ending Capital + Item K Allocable Liabilities = Outside Tax Basis (IRC §705 & §752).
Form 1120-S: S-Corporation Compliance & Basis Pass-Through Core
  • Officer Reasonable Compensation: Mandating W-2 wages on Line 7 prior to shareholder distributions to prevent payroll tax evasion (Rev. Rul. 74-44).
  • Form 7203 Stock Basis Ordering: BOY Basis + Nonseparately & Separately Stated Income – Distributions – Nondeductible Expenses – Losses (Treas. Reg. §1.1367-1).
  • Shareholder Debt Basis: Creating basis only through direct shareholder loans; applying income to restore debt basis before stock basis (IRC §1367(b)(2)).
  • Schedule M-2 AAA vs. OAA: Segregating taxable earnings in the Accumulated Adjustments Account from tax-exempt items in the Other Adjustments Account.
  • Excess Distribution Capital Gain: Recognizing capital gain on Schedule D/Form 8949 when cash distributions exceed available stock basis (IRC §1368(b)(2)).
  • Loss Suspension Accounting: Tracking pro-rata suspended ordinary and capital losses when stock and debt basis drop to $0.
Form 1040: Flow-Through & Loss Limitation Waterfall Individual Core
  • Schedule E (Part II) Ingestion: Separating non-passive trade/business income from passive rental real estate from K-1 Box 1 and Box 2.
  • Tier 1 — Outside / Stock Basis Limitation: Disallowing K-1 losses that exceed basis; maintaining indefinite carryover workpapers (IRC §704(d) / §1366(d)).
  • Tier 2 — At-Risk Limitation (Form 6198): Testing loss deductions against amounts at risk; disallowing nonrecourse debt losses unless qualified real estate debt (IRC §465).
  • Tier 3 — Passive Activity Loss (Form 8582): Restricting passive losses to passive income; evaluating active participation rental real estate allowances (IRC §469).
  • Tier 4 — Excess Business Loss (Form 461): Enforcing the statutory cap on aggregate net business losses against non-business income.
  • Self-Employment Tax Verification: Confirming SE tax is paid on General Partner K-1 income, but excluded on S-Corp K-1 distributions and passive LP income (IRC §1402).
