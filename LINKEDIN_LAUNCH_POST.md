## 🛠️ Part 3: How to Capture Professional Video & Zoom Screen Recordings

Since you already recorded your vocals, here are the top 3 best ways to produce this professional demo video:

### Option 1: Screen Studio (The #1 Video Tool for Tech LinkedIn / Twitter Demos)
- **Why It's Famous**: Screen Studio automatically detects mouse clicks and **automatically adds smooth cinema-grade camera zooms, silky cursor motion blur, and professional background paddings** with zero editing skills required!
- **How to Use**:
  1. Open Screen Studio.
  2. Click through your website (`returns.html` ➔ Entity Tabs ➔ `[Open Return]` ➔ `[Audit & Verify]`).
  3. Drag your recorded vocal `.mp3`/`.wav` file into the audio timeline.
  4. Screen Studio auto-zooms to every click and matches the audio pacing.
  5. Export 4K 60fps MP4!

### Option 2: OBS Studio + CapCut Desktop (100% Free & RTX 3080 Hardware NVENC)
- **Step 1: Record 60fps Screen via OBS Studio**:
  - Run OBS Studio on your PC (it uses your RTX 3080 NVENC encoder for crisp 4K recording with zero lag).
  - Record yourself navigating `returns.html` and `index.html`.
- **Step 2: CapCut Desktop (Free)**:
  - Import your OBS recording + your recorded vocal audio.
  - Use CapCut's **"Auto-Captions"** (adds trendy animated subtitles so people can read your post even with sound muted).
  - Use keyframes to add smooth slow-zooms on key buttons (`[+ Create Tax Return]`, the green RTX badge, and Qwen 14B review pane).

### Option 3: Automated Browser Screenshot / Recording via Antigravity (Playwright / Puppeteer)
- We can write a automated Python script using Playwright in your repo:
  - It boots headless Chromium.
  - Takes pixel-perfect 4K screenshots of `returns.html`, the modals, and `index.html`.
  - Automatically records an MP4 video of the browser clicking through the tabs!
